Research

Materials research across experiment, computation and sustainability.

Our group works at the intersection of materials science, chemistry, engineering and machine learning to develop and understand polymer-based systems.

Research themes

Core areas of investigation.

We combine experimental development with computational and data-driven approaches to accelerate materials innovation.

01

Polymer Composites

Development, processing and characterization of polymer composite systems for improved mechanical performance, functionality and engineering use.

02

Sustainable Materials

Development of renewable, biobased and circular material systems, including sustainable polymer composites and products derived from locally available resources.

03

Machine Learning for Materials

Application of machine learning to predict composite properties, understand response behavior and accelerate materials design and discovery.

04

Advanced Manufacturing

Investigation of processing and manufacturing methods that enable scalable, high-performance and sustainable polymer-based products.

How we work

Connecting research across scales.

Our projects move from materials development and characterization through modeling, prediction and application.

01

Develop

Create new polymer formulations, composites and sustainable materials systems.

02

Characterize

Study mechanical, physical and functional properties using experimental techniques.

03

Model & Predict

Use computational and machine-learning tools to investigate structure-property relationships.

04

Translate

Connect research outcomes with manufacturing, industry and practical engineering challenges.
