Biography

Research leadership rooted in materials engineering and application.

Dr. Eric Asare is a Lecturer in Materials Engineering at KNUST with expertise in polymer composite design, processing and characterization for specific engineering applications. He has more than five years of industrial experience and has worked with companies and factories in the United States, Asia and Europe.

His work also includes research commercialization, peer-reviewed publication and conference presentation, with a focus on turning materials research into useful engineering outcomes.

Research interests

Smart multifunctional composites Polymer processing Materials characterization Nanomaterials Polymer composites

Research group

Dr. Asare leads research focused on smart multifunctional composites, polymer processing, sustainable materials development and the application of engineering tools to improve polymer-based systems.
