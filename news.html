New publication in Royal Society Open Science

Our paper, “Synergistic effects of micro- and macro-sized palm kernel shell fillers on the tensile properties of HDPE composites,” was published in Royal Society Open Science.

Vice Chancellor's Excellence Award

Congratulations to Jephtah on receiving a gold award at the Vice Chancellor's Excellence Award, recognizing innovation and excellence in project work.

SEESA project successfully completed

The group completed its SEESA project focused on developing affordable and sustainable sanitary pads, supporting the broader goal of improving health and well-being through materials innovation.
