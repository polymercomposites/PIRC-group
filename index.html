Research approach

Building materials knowledge across scales.

PIRC brings together materials science, engineering, computation and sustainability to solve challenging problems involving polymer-based systems.

01

Experiment

Develop, process and characterize polymer and composite materials.

02

Model

Use computational tools to investigate material behavior across multiple length scales.

03

Predict

Apply data-driven and machine-learning approaches to accelerate materials development.

04

Translate

Connect research discoveries with engineering, manufacturing and industrial applications.

Capabilities

Research and consultancy

PIRC supports academic and industry partners across materials development, characterization, computational analysis and engineering problem-solving.

Materials Selection

Selection and optimization of polymer systems based on application, performance and processing requirements.

Materials Development

Development of new polymer formulations, composite systems and sustainable material solutions.

Processing & Manufacturing

Investigation of processing routes and manufacturing strategies for polymer-based products.

Characterization & Testing

Evaluation of mechanical, physical and functional material properties.

Computational Analysis

Simulation, modeling and data-driven analysis of materials and structure-property relationships.

Industry Collaboration

Collaborative research and technical support for materials challenges and product innovation.
