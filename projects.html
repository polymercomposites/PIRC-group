Selected projects

Documented research activity.

The projects below reflect research and funding information currently documented by the group. Project details can be expanded as new milestones and outcomes are published.

01 · Completed 2024

Affordable and Sustainable Sanitary Pads

A completed SEESA-supported project focused on developing affordable and sustainable sanitary pads, aligned with PIRC’s work on renewable materials and practical products for underserved communities.

Support: Strengthening Engineering Ecosystem in Sub-Saharan Africa (SEESA)

02 · Funded research theme

Novel Polymer Materials Development

Research support directed toward the development and investigation of novel polymer materials, contributing to the group’s broader work in polymer processing, composites and functional materials.

Support: Strengthening Engineering Ecosystem in Sub-Saharan Africa (SEESA)

03 · KReF-supported

Sustainable Biocomposite Development

Research focused on sustainable biocomposite materials and the use of renewable resources in polymer systems. PIRC received KNUST Research Fund support for this area.

Support: KNUST Research Fund (KReF)
